The free, private way to run your entire job search

Career Copilot is a free desktop app for job seekers. It scans job boards, scores every role against your resume, generates tailored ATS-friendly CVs, and tracks your applications in a drag-and-drop pipeline — all on your own computer.

Everything your job hunt needs, in one window

A job board aggregator, resume/CV builder, and application tracker rolled into one private app.

🔎

Scan & score job boards

Pulls fresh roles from 10 free job boards daily — searching each location you care about, not just remote — and scores every one 0–100 against your profile: skills, seniority, work arrangement, visa-friendliness, and domain fit.

📄

Tailored ATS-friendly CVs

Generate a Word CV for any job, built only from your real profile facts and reworded for that role. A verification pass flags anything not traceable to you.

🗂️

Kanban application tracker

Track every job from Sourced → Applied → Screening → Interview → Offer with draggable cards, a filterable table, and an analytics view of your funnel.

🌐

Apply from inside the app

Open any listing in a built-in browser and apply yourself — the app never auto-submits, so you stay clear of bot-submission filters.

🎯

Add your own sources

Point it at any RSS feed, a Greenhouse or Lever company board, or a single job by pasting its URL. Remote, hybrid, or on-site — you choose.

🔒

Private by design

Everything runs on your machine. No sign-up, no subscription, no paid API, and your resume and applications never leave your computer.

How it works

From a blank profile to a tracked application in four steps.

Import your profile

Paste in or upload your resume — Career Copilot turns it into a structured profile it will never invent facts beyond.

Scan and shortlist

Run a scan and get scored, de-duplicated roles with red-flag warnings, so you spend time only on the ones that fit.

Generate a tailored CV

One click produces an ATS-friendly CV ordered and reworded for that specific job — honestly, from your own experience.

Apply and track

Apply in the built-in browser, then move the card through your pipeline and watch your response rate in analytics.

Free for your job search

Career Copilot is free to use for your personal job search under a personal-use license. The full source is public on GitHub — read every line, run it yourself, and adapt it to your needs. No premium tier, no data harvesting, and nothing to sign up for.
