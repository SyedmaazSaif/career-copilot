WINDOWS ONLY
============

Nothing here runs on a Mac. On this Mac, go up one folder and open
Career Copilot.app.

On a Windows PC:

  First time:        double-click   Setup on Windows.bat
                     (installs what's needed, puts a shortcut on the Desktop)

  Every time after:  double-click   Start Career Copilot.vbs
                     or the "Career Copilot" shortcut on your Desktop.
                     No console window appears.

  install-windows.bat   only for putting the app on a PC that does not have
                        it yet -- it downloads a fresh copy from GitHub.
